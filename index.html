About

I am Gianmarco La Rosa. I received my Ph.D. in Mathematics and Computational Sciences from the University of Palermo on February 26, 2024. My doctoral research focused on non-commutative and non-associative algebra, with particular attention to Lie algebras, Leibniz algebras, derivations, and biderivations.

I am currently a research fellow at the University of Palermo. My present work is connected with mathematical models for explainable artificial intelligence, especially fuzzy ontologies, aggregation methods, and knowledge representation. I also continue my research in algebra and geometry, and I am interested in fuzzy algebra and fuzzy topological structures.
